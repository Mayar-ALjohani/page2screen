About Us

Our Story

We are two friends who love watching movies and reading books. Every time we watched a movie, we wondered if it was based on a book and where we could find it. That is why we created Page2Screen.

Why use our website?

  • Movie and book info in one page
  • Know the author before you watch
  • Links to stores to buy the book
  • Save time instead of searching many websites

Our Goals

  1. Give full info about movies (story, duration, cast).
  2. Show the book behind each movie and its author.
  3. Help users find where to buy the book.
