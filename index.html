What is Page2Screen?

Many movies are based on books, but the info about them is in different websites. Page2Screen puts everything in one place: the movie story, duration and cast, and also the book behind it, its author, number of pages and where to buy it.

Why use our website?

  • Movie and book info in one page
  • Know the author before you watch
  • Links to stores to buy the book
  • Save time instead of searching many websites
